6. Fill an event to capacity and attempt another registration: full-event exception is shown.
7. Cancel an existing registration: registration removed and one seat released.
8. Enter invalid menu input: program reports numeric-option requirement without terminating.
9. Exit and restart: records remain available, demonstrating file persistence.
10. Attempt unknown participant/event IDs: registration rejected.
